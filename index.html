3 · Review limitations
This tool compares only evidence it can identify in the supported document format. It does not perform OCR, infer a panel schedule, verify listing or seismic certificates, or independently confirm the selected breaker's interrupting rating. Catalog examples are not approved equipment. SCCR is not interchangeable with breaker interrupting capacity. Confirm all drawing requirements and actual selected equipment with the project team.
